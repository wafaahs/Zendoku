Zendoku Grove

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

In short: Zendoku Grove does not collect, store, share or sell any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. The developer never receives any information about you or how you use the app.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, statistics, achievements and the puzzle in progress are saved in a file on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This data stays on your device and isn’t sent to the developer.

iCloud sync

If you’re signed in to iCloud, the app uses Apple’s iCloud key-value storage to keep your progress in sync between your own iPhone, iPad and Mac. This data is stored in your iCloud account under Apple’s privacy policy. The developer cannot access it. To stop syncing, turn off iCloud for the app in your device settings. To erase your progress everywhere, use Settings → Reset All Progress in the app.

Notifications

Optional reminders are scheduled locally on your device. They’re off by default, and no notification data leaves your device.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future version adds new features, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a new date.
